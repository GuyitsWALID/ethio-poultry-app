# Sales Data Discovery Gate

System B is intentionally on hold while System A reaches operational completion.

Profit-per-flock remains deferred until the team decides how sales data will be gathered and reconciled. The required decision is whether sales enters the platform through:

- Manual sales entry
- POS transactions
- Invoice/proforma workflow
- CRM/package-sale conversion
- Accounting import
- A hybrid of the above

Until this decision is made, System A should only calculate operational and cost-side metrics such as feed cost per egg, cost per bird, stock levels, mortality, health compliance, and production performance.

Do not use placeholder revenue or fake sales assumptions to calculate profit.
