# Complete Operational Planning and Recording Gaps

## Why
History simplified navigation but left planning and monthly expense tools in
legacy pages. Today also lacks verified first-day opening counts and explicit
egg-sale source allocation.

## What Changes
- Establish placement-day bird counts and governed recording-start baselines.
- Allocate a farm's egg sale explicitly across one or more producing flocks.
- Add Scheduler under More and complete dated work inside Today.
- Add Expenses & purchases under More, sharing authoritative forms with Today.
- Extend bilingual, offline, reconciliation, reporting and lifecycle coverage.

## Confirmed Product Decisions
- CEO and managers create schedules directly within authorized scope.
- Managers edit only their own plans; CEO controls CEO-created plans.
- All schedules target named flocks and stop with their archived/completed identity.
- Due work requires completion, reasoned deferral or reasoned skipping before Finish day.
- Combined egg sales may span flocks but not farms.
- Existing farms use a CEO-approved physical starting count rather than invented history.
- Expenses/purchases reuse existing records; no purchase-order/payables system.
